### About Me

Hi, I’m **@Liendea**!

I'm a **developer and designer** who thrives at the intersection of clean, well-structured code and intuitive, beautiful design. For me, development isn't just about making things work—it's about creating seamless user experiences that look great and are a joy to use.

* 👀 **Interests:** Snowboarding 🏂, Golf ⛳, Nature 🌲, Animals 🐾, and Web Development 💻
* 🌱 **Currently learning:** Three.js & 3D Web Animations ✨
* 💞️ **Looking to collaborate on:** Web design and web development projects!
* ☕ **Fueled by:** Coffee, good design, problem-solving, and creative challenges.
